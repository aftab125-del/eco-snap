Environmental Hacks
Bad air, heatwaves, floods, water shortage and waste are problems we see every day. In this hackathon, you pick one of these problems and build something that helps solve it. It can be a problem in your city, your college or your home.
The hackathon runs online Thursday to Sunday, with an optional build day at DTU, Delhi, on Saturday, Oct 10. Limited seats, and you get workshops, project feedback, time with the Amazon team, and swag.

04 / Judging
What judges look for
We are looking for projects that make a real difference to the air people breathe, the water they depend on, and the waste and energy their cities run on.
01
Idea and Impact
Pick a track and find the problem inside it.
Does it fix a real environmental problem? And what changes for the people living with it?
A small problem solved well beats a big one solved vaguely.
02
Built on AWS
Build locally with AWS open-source tools, or deploy on AWS services. Start on the AWS Free Tier, which gives a new account up to $200 in credits.
Using an AWS open-source project or AWS services is mandatory to win a prize.
03
Design and usability
The people living with the problem have to be able to use the fix: clear to pick up, easy to get around, and built for where they are.
Would someone who isn't on your team know what to do with it?
04
The execution
Does it work? Not perfect, not polished. Working. One feature that runs beats five that almost do.
05
The demo video
Three minutes, recorded, to show what it does, who it is for, and where AWS fits. There is no live demo, so the video is what the judges see.
We judge what you built, not what you spent. Projects built locally and projects deployed on AWS are scored with the same care, and each track is judged on its own.
05 / FAQ
Got questions?
What is the theme?
The environment. Pick one of three tracks, Air, Heat and Water, or Waste and Energy, and build something that fixes a real problem inside it: bad air on the way to school, a street that floods every monsoon, a building that could run on its own roof. Start with a problem you have seen yourself.
Do I have to pick a track?
Yes, pick the one your project fits best. Each track has its own winner, and the top 10 students for the fast-track interviews at Amazon are picked from all three tracks together.
What happened to Build It and Ship It?
They're not tracks this time. They're the two ways to build: locally with AWS open-source tools, or deployed on AWS. Any team in any track can pick either one.
Do I need to know AWS?
No. The Build It stack needs no AWS account at all, and the resources on this page get you started on the rest.
How do I build on AWS without paying?
Start with the AWS Free Tier. A new AWS account comes with up to $200 in free credits, on top of the services that are always free, and that is enough to build and deploy a hackathon project. Using it is completely valid for every track.
See what the free tier covers
Do I need a credit card?
No. Building locally needs nothing at all. Deploying needs an AWS account, and the free tier makes that free: debit cards and RuPay work for the signup, and the verification charge is about ₹2.
How do the fast-track interviews at Amazon work?
The judges put the top projects forward to Amazon's University Talent Acquisition Team, and those students skip the screening round: the project stands in for it. Top 10 students from top projects go forward at each event, picked from all three tracks together. The roles are a 6-month internship at Amazon and full-time positions. Winning a track is neither required nor enough, and the full terms are in the tour's rules.
Read the rules
Can I participate online?
Yes, fully. Same theme, same judging, same prizes, and no cap on numbers.
Where is the build day?
At Delhi Technological University (DTU), Shahbad Daulatpur, Main Bawana Road, Delhi 110042, on Saturday, Oct 10, from 8 AM to 8 PM. Online runs the whole four days from anywhere in India.

Does joining in person help my chances?
No. The hackathon runs online from Thursday to Sunday. The Delhi day is optional and has limited seats, and being there doesn't add to your score.
Where can I read up before the weekend?
We write about the tour as it goes: guides on the services you'll reach for, recaps of past events, and build notes from the people who were there. Everything tagged aws on the blog is about this tour.

Who can participate?

How big can a team be?
One to four people.
Is it free?
Yes. Free to register, free to take part.
I took part in First Commit. Can I enter this one too?
Yes. One tour registration covers every event, and entering one never rules out another. Check in here to enter this one.

Yes, everyone who submits a project will get a certificate, and the winning teams will also get a certificate.
Can I start building before the event?
Learn and practise as much as you want. Project work starts when the clock does.
